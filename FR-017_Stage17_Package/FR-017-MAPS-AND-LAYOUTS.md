## No-Pressure Zones

- Inner elbow crease.
- Inner forearm.
- Wrist bones.
- Radius and ulna ridge peaks.
- Palm path.
- Grip path.
- Any glove-removal or wrist-removal path.

## Provisional Interface Zones

- Outer forearm cable sleeve.
- Outer/dorsal frame-alignment tabs.
- Outer/dorsal quick-release pull or press control.
- Broad padded strap zones.
- Non-compressive latch and pressure sensor placeholders.

## Redesign Triggers

- Strap crosses inner elbow crease.
- Strap presses wrist bones.
- Cable crosses inner elbow, wrist bones, palm, or grip path.
- Sensor sits in a pressure zone.
- Release is hidden by frame or armor.
- Cuff prevents glove or wrist assembly removal.
- Cuff migration reaches elbow or wrist clearance zones.
